How to Join a Crew

Great films rely on strong teams. Whether you are a screenwriter looking for a director or a sound mixer seeking your next set, our monthly pitch nights match local talent with active productions.

  • Writers & Directors: Pitch short scripts for seasonal collective productions.
  • Cinematographers & Sound: Browse open crew calls and share gear resources.
  • Actors & Editors: Submit reels to our member directory for upcoming casting call and post-production roles.
